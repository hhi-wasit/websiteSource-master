### Conference presentations

During the PhD period, I presented research at the following international conferences and seminars:

- **14th IMT-GT UNINET Bioscience Conference 2025** — Oral Presenter, February 2025.
- **1st Kedah International Health Conference 2025** — Oral Presenter, July 2025.
- **4th International Conference on Medical and Health Sciences**, held in conjunction with the ASEAN Research Collaborative Fund Symposium and the 25th National Conference on Medical and Health Sciences — Poster Presenter, September 2025.
- **Social Science Postgraduate International Seminar**, Universiti Sains Malaysia — Oral Presenter, September 2025.

### Ongoing works

My current research activities include a study of the **spatiotemporal dynamics of four zoonotic public health threats in Wasit Governorate, Iraq, from 2022 to 2025**, using Bayesian hierarchical and negative binomial regression analysis.

I am also working on the **cross-cultural adaptation and psychometric validation of an Arabic version of the Knowledge, Attitudes, and Practices questionnaire for breast cancer screening**.
