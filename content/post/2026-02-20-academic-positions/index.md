### Lecturer · Higher Health Institute of Wasit, Iraq
**2025–present**

I teach undergraduate students in emergency health, public health, and epidemiology. My responsibilities include lectures on infectious disease surveillance and research methods, student research supervision, academic mentoring, curriculum development, and participation in academic activities.

### Community Health Specialist · Public Health Department of Wasit, Iraq
**2018–2021 · Maternal and Child Health Division**

I conducted training for health workers and doctors on Antenatal Care, Integrated Management of Neonatal and Child Health, and Emergency Obstetric and Neonatal Care.

### Public Health Officer · Public Health Department of Wasit, Iraq
**2010–2015**

I worked across the Communicable Diseases and Health Visitor Units, the Medical and Pharmacy Units at Wasit health centre, and the Antenatal and Postnatal Care Unit.

### PhD Researcher · Universiti Sains Malaysia
**2021–2025**

I designed and conducted a cross-sectional epidemiological study on community-based infectious disease surveillance in Malaysia, developed and validated questionnaire instruments, applied advanced quantitative and psychometric methods, and produced peer-reviewed scholarly outputs during the PhD period.
