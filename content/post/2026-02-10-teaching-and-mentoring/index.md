I am a Lecturer at the Higher Health Institute of Wasit, Iraq. My teaching and academic activities are centred on helping students develop practical public health knowledge, research skills, and the ability to interpret epidemiological evidence.

### Undergraduate teaching

I teach undergraduate students in **emergency health, public health, and epidemiology**. I deliver lectures on infectious disease surveillance and research methods, supervise student research, provide academic mentoring, and contribute to curriculum development and wider academic activities.

### Health-worker training

Before joining the Higher Health Institute of Wasit, I worked as a Community Health Specialist in the Maternal and Child Health Division of the Public Health Department of Wasit. I conducted training for health workers and doctors on Antenatal Care, Integrated Management of Neonatal and Child Health, and Emergency Obstetric and Neonatal Care.

I am also a WHO-certified e-instructor with a Training of Trainers qualification.
