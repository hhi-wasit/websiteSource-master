My doctoral research examined community-based surveillance of infectious diseases, community engagement, questionnaire development, and the social and behavioural factors associated with participation.

### Published articles

- Hasan, A. A., Ghazali, A. K., Bachok, N., Yaacob, N. M., Hairon, S. M., Nadir, N. A. M., & Shukri, F. M. (2025). **Modeling the determinants of community engagement in community-based surveillance of infectious diseases: Applying the Health Belief Model.** *Journal of Public Health Research*, 14, 1–10.

- Hasan, A. A., Ghazali, A. K., Bachok, N., Yaacob, N. M., Hairon, S. M., Nadir, N. A. M., & Shukri, F. (2025). **A confirmatory analysis of the knowledge, attitudes, and perceptions questionnaire for community-based surveillance of infectious diseases: A cross-sectional study in Malaysia.** *Osong Public Health and Research Perspectives*, 16, 229–241.

- Hasan, A. A., Ghazali, A. K., Bachok, N., Yaacob, N. M., Hairon, S. M., Nadir, N. A. M., & Shukri, F. M. (2025). **Community participation in community-based surveillance of infectious diseases: A structural equation modeling approach based on the theory of reasoned action.** *International Journal of One Health*, 11(1), 178–185.

- Hasan, A. A., Bachok, N., Yaacob, N. M., Hairon, S. M., Nadir, N. A. M., & Ghazali, A. K. (2025). **Assessing community leaders’ knowledge, attitudes, and perceptions on community-based surveillance of infectious diseases: Development and validation of a questionnaire.** *Medical Journal of Babylon*, 22, 156–163.

- Hasan, A. A., Ghazali, A. K., Bachok, N., Yaacob, N. M., Hairon, S. M., & Nadira, A. (2026). **Role of self-efficacy in mediating the effect between perceived barriers and benefits and intention to be involved in community-based surveillance of infectious diseases.** In *Building inclusive sustainability: Pathways to policy, community development & environmental innovation* (pp. 61–70). Universiti Pendidikan Sultan Idris.

- Hasan, A. A., Ghazali, A. K., Bachok, N. A., Yaacob, N. M., Hairon, S. M., Nadir, N. A. M., & Shukri, F. M. (2026). **Modeling social and behavioral factors associated with community participation in community-based surveillance of communicable diseases: A cross-sectional study in Kelantan, Malaysia.** *Osong Public Health and Research Perspectives*.
